# MR.QUEEN
## Overview
*Mr.Queen* is a South Korean Historical comedy-drama series that been released in 2020.
The story follows **Jang Bong-hwan**, amoderdn day chef who mysteriously find his soul trapped in the body of **Queen Cheorin** during the Joseon Dynasty.
This series beautifully mixes humor and romance.

---

## Genre
- Historical
- Fantasy
- Comedy
- Romance

---

## Main Characters
1. **Queen Cheorin / Jang Bong-hwan (Shin Hye-sun)** A queen with the soul of a modern man, bringing chaos and laughter to the royal palace.
2. **King Cheoljong (Kim Jung-hyun)** The seemingly gentle king who hides his intelligence and ambition behind a calm exterior.
3. **Kim Jwa-geun (Kim Tae-woo)** A powerful nobleman seeking to control the throne.
4. **Jo Hwa-jin (Seol In-ah)** The royal concubine who holds deep affection for the king.

---

## Why I love this series
1. The **unique body-swap concept** between a modern man and a Joseon queen creates hilarious and unpredictable moments.
2. The **chemistry** between Shin Hye-sun and Kim Jung-hyun is outstanding.
3. The **cinematography** and **costume design** bring the Joseon era to life.
